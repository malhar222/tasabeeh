Questions

Is Tasabeeh free?

Yes, dhikr itself is always free: the misbaha, the Action Button and every built-in list. Tasabeeh Pro adds the after-prayer reminder after every adhan, the next-prayer widget, fine misbaha materials, unlimited adhkar of your own and your full history. You can try the reminder and the widget free for your first seven days.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is one payment through Apple, yours forever.

Where do the adhkar come from?

From Hisn al-Muslim, each with its source in the books of Sunnah. If you spot a mistake, tell us on the Support page.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start counting.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There are no servers, analytics or tracking, and prayer times are worked out on the phone. Read the privacy policy.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The Action Button is on iPhone 15 Pro and later; Control Center and the widgets work on every model.
