Privacy Policy

Tasabeeh (تسابيح) · Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

Tasabeeh (تسابيح) does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you create in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved locally on your iPhone so the app can work. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active purchase.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Tasabeeh (تسابيح) does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
